Starting points for the design session

  1. The big number font has no letters. On the fēnix 7 Pro, FONT_NUMBER_MEDIUM only holds digits, space and # % + - . / : °. The Status pages (LOCKED, OFF, the km unit, the — placeholder), the Find my car distance unit and the °C of the target temperature all use it. Switch on Font gaps to see which characters fall outside the font. What the watch draws for them needs a check on the device. Status · Find my car · Target temp
  2. Hint lines run off the round edge. Bottom hints drawn with plain drawText at y = height − 20/22 only have about 55 px of visible width there. Status, Charging and Find my car lose most of their hint.
  3. The home screen's hint is pushed up into the grid. TextBlock.drawFittedLine moves "UP charging - DOWN status" up until it fits the circle, which lands it at y ≈ 208, on the scroll arrow and the partly visible fourth tile row. It is the same dark grey as the tiles, so where it crosses one it disappears. Home
  4. The status strip on top of home is too wide for the circle and shows the raw enum (READY_FOR_CHARGING). Home · Glance
  5. Scrolling the tile grid draws over the strip. The grid does not clip, so a scrolled row sits on top of the battery/lock strip and a fourth row peeks out at the bottom. Home, scrolled
  6. The map has no route to navigation. MENU on the map does nothing; "Navigate to car" lives on the bearing screen only (see research note of today). Map

Drawn in the browser from a JavaScript port of each view's onUpdate(). Letter shapes use Roboto / Roboto Condensed at the size where their digits match the watch font; every character is placed on the watch font's own advance width, so line lengths, wrapping and clipping match the device. Guides show the 8 px round-margin that TextBlock keeps and the 52 px tile rows.
